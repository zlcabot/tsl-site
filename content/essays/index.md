Longer walks through one idea at a time, dated and tended. When the
letters begin, what goes out by email lives here afterward, and the
archive stays public.

- [[what-this-work-does|What this work does]]: an orientation. One
  question to carry anywhere, what happens when you ask it of the
  sciences and the traditions, and what it would take to prove this
  wrong.
- [[what-is-time|What is time?]]: not a river, not a line, not a
  container. Happenings keeping time, together.
