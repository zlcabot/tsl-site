Longer walks through one idea at a time, dated and tended. Each carries
a short opening for reading straight through and a precise second half
for anyone who wants the argument with its apparatus exposed. When the
letters begin, what goes out by email lives here afterward, and the
archive stays public.

## Newest

- [[what-this-work-does|The death exemption: what can you not let die?]]
  · 21 August 2026. One question to ask of any science, any tradition,
  or yourself, what happens when you ask it, and what it would take to
  prove this work wrong.
- [[what-is-time|What is time?]] · 9 August 2026. Not a river, not a
  line, not a container. Happenings keeping time, together.

## By topic

Every essay carries one to four topics. A topic gathers everything on
the site that touches it, essays and terms and wiki entries together.

- [Mortality](/tags/mortality): finitude, death, grief, perishing, and
  completion.
- [Time](/tags/time): temporality, timing, duration, and temporal
  plurality.
- [Tending](/tags/tending): the work's central verb and its direct
  elaborations.
- [Consciousness](/tags/consciousness): awareness, reflexivity, and
  selfhood.
- [Agency](/tags/agency): action, responsibility, freedom, and response.
- [Practice](/tags/practice): exercises, disciplines, and lived ways of
  working.
- [Ecology](/tags/ecology): living systems, land, and interdependence.
- [Modernity](/tags/modernity): acceleration, mechanism, progress, and
  its endings.
- [Politics](/tags/politics): institutions, power, labor, and collective
  decision.
