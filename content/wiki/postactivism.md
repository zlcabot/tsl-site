Postactivism is [[bayo-akomolafe|Báyò Akómoláfé]]'s name for the cracks in which established modes of political response lose their fit. The “post” does not announce the end of activism or a superior program that comes after it. It marks interruptions where the inherited problem, agent, goal, and solution can no longer continue unchanged.

• • • —

## When response belongs to the problem

Postactivism asks a recursive question: what if the way we respond to a problem helps reproduce the conditions called the problem?

A movement may oppose extraction while reproducing mastery. Urgency may narrow perception to familiar interventions. Expertise may preserve the fantasy that the world waits passively for a human solver.

Cracks redistribute agency. Bodies, infrastructures, ecologies, histories, microbes, and accidents can change what the situation is. Slowing down becomes one way to become available to participants and possibilities excluded by the established frame.

Making sanctuary names the work of composing with damage, grief, and strangeness when the old world cannot simply be restored.

## The reading here

Postactivism and [[tending]] both refuse a sovereign agent outside the field of response. The responder is formed and changed by responding.

Tending does not make cracks or slowness intrinsically good. Different situations call for different timings. It also retains unequal responsibility: many beings may participate in a situation without bearing the same obligations or possessing the same alternatives.

The pressure test is direct harm. Can postactivism recognize when delay protects power and immediate intervention is required?
