Four ways in, depending on where you're coming from. Each is a short
walk that ends inside the web of notes: different doors, one body
of work.

- [For the curious](general) — no prerequisites; begin with what happens.
- [For practitioners](practitioner) — begin with practice, meet the ideas after.
- [For philosophers](philosopher) — begin with the metaphysics.
- [For scientists](scientist) — begin with the instruments and the evidence.
