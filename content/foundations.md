The basic ideas of this work, briefly, and beneath them the six
axioms it stands on, stated plainly so that any reader, human or
machine, can catch this work drifting from itself. Nothing here
needs to be believed. Every idea is offered as a way of reading, to
be tried rather than accepted.

• • • —

## The ideas

Everything is happening. What we call things are happenings with
their own pace; a mountain is a very slow act, a thought a very
fast one. And a happening does not sit _in_ time, because its
happening _is_ its time. The whole of it lives at [[tending]].

One verb, three ways. To tend is to co-create what is happening,
and _extend, intend, attend_ are that one verb, three ways.
[[extending|Extending]] is the out of: intensification, the
eruption into form. [[intending|Intending]] is the with: the
membrane, different happenings holding one shared while.
[[attending|Attending]] is the toward: the arc, directionality
before any plan, flowering at the human scale as care.

Each act happens once. No happening repeats. What completes does
not come back; it becomes what later happenings tend out of. The
page for this is [[the-once]].

There is no reality outside the tending. To check whether a reality
stands behind all the happening, you would have to step outside
every happening and look, and the stepping and looking would
themselves be tendings. The real is met within the tending, where
it answers back, resists, surprises. Met, not conjured.

Nothing happens alone. Every act arises with others, out of
others, toward others. There is no self-made happening and no view
from nowhere.

Consciousness is the fold. Not a spark inside the skull but a
happening that includes its own tending among what it tends, care
that also cares for its caring: [[consciousness]].

Agency is a doing, never a doer. The fold at the human scale, the
act that knows it acts and knows it completes. Freedom lives here,
not as escape from the happening but as the freedom to inhabit it,
right where you are: [[agency]].

## The six axioms

These are the commitments underneath the ideas, numbered and
exposed. Each is held as revisable, and each states, in the fuller
record, exactly what would defeat it. They are published for a
working reason: these are the guardrails that catch drift, whether
the author's, a reader's, or an AI's, and guardrails only work in
the open.

### 1 · Events, not substances

Eventing, and multiple eventings. Every event arises on the deaths
of prior events, and every event dies. There is no substrate
beneath the happening and no completed totality containing it,
never one, never all. This catches talk sliding back into permanent
stuff, hidden foundations, or "the universe" as a finished
container.

### 2 · Three ways, none reducible

Every happening tends all three ways at once; no way reduces to the
others, and none is the master. The count is not sacred. A genuine
fourth way that earned its place would be admitted, and an
incumbent that failed would be removed, because the test cuts both
directions. This catches any one way quietly absolutized into the
whole, whether pure matter, pure mind, or pure relation.

### 3 · The once

Every event completes, once. Completing makes it definite, but
definite is not past. Nothing is stored and nothing is transferred;
the completed once is what later happenings tend out of, each in
their own way. This catches the dodge into persistence, resting on
what supposedly endures instead of tending what is happening.

### 4 · Every reading is from somewhere

There is no view from nowhere. A claim becomes checkable only when
it names where it reads from: this scale, this practice, this
instrument, this life. Unlocated claims are not yet true or false;
they are not yet anything. This catches the god's-eye sentence, the
most common drift in philosophy and the most common drift in
machines trained on it.

### 5 · No single scale closes the event

The same happening reads differently at different scales, and no
scale is the real one. A cell's reading does not refute a galaxy's;
a physics does not retire a practice. What one scale calls a thing,
another rightly calls a family of acts. This catches the mistake of
crowning one scale's grammar, usually measurement's, as the way
things are.

### 6 · Describing is making

Every description is an act, a making rather than a window, and
this articulation is no exception. These pages are a score, not the
music, and the map here is offered as a way, never the way. This
catches the final drift, the framework mistaking itself for the
reality it reads, the one trap every system falls into at the end,
refused here at the start.

## How this stays honest

Three disciplines, kept in public. Claims carry their dates and are
revised in place, and the record of changes is open. Retractions
are carried at the same weight as confirmations, so what this work
got wrong stays visibly wrong instead of quietly deleted. And
arguments that failed stay failed; a defeated argument may not
sneak back in through a side door wearing new words. The aim is not
to be unassailable. The aim is to be able to lose, and to say so,
because that is what separates a living philosophy from a monument.
