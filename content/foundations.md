The basic ideas of this work, each in a few sentences, each opening
onto its own page. This is the map; the paths (arriving) are the
walks. Nothing here needs to be believed — every idea is offered as a
way of reading, to be tried rather than accepted.

• • • —

## Everything is happening

What we call things are happenings with their own pace — a mountain a
very slow act, a thought a very fast one. Events, not substances: the
happening is what there is. And a happening does not sit *in* time;
its happening *is* its time. The whole of it: [[tending]].

## One verb, three ways

To tend is to co-create what is happening — and *extend, intend,
attend* are that one verb, three ways. [[extending|Extending]], *out
of*: intensification, the eruption into form. [[intending|Intending]],
*with*: the membrane, different happenings holding one shared while.
[[attending|Attending]], *toward*: the arc — directionality before
any plan, flowering at the human scale as care. Three ways of one
act; none reduces to the others.

## Each act happens once

No happening repeats. What completes does not come back — it becomes
what later happenings tend out of. This is the work's deepest claim,
and the one modern life is best organized to avoid hearing:
[[the-once]].

## No reality outside the tending

To check whether a reality stands behind all the happening, you would
have to step outside every happening and look — and the stepping and
looking would themselves be tendings. So: no reality outside the
tending, and the real met within it — answering back, resisting,
surprising. Met, not conjured. See [[tending]], "Tending reality."

## Nothing happens alone

Every act arises with others, out of others, toward others. There is
no self-made happening and no view from nowhere — every reading of
the world is itself an act inside it, from somewhere, and honest
about that.

## Consciousness is the fold

Not a spark inside the skull: consciousness is tending turned
reflexive — a happening that includes its own tending among what it
tends. Care that also cares for its caring: [[consciousness]].

## Agency is a doing, never a doer

No little commander sits inside. Agency is the fold at the human
scale — the act that knows it acts and knows it completes. Freedom
lives here: not escape from the happening, but the freedom to inhabit
it, right where you are: [[agency]].

## A way, not the way

All of this is one articulation, made from somewhere, able to be
wrong, revised in public. It refuses to be a system that closes. The
invitation stands at the end of [[tending]]: assume it, and tend —
and see what becomes sayable that was not sayable before.
