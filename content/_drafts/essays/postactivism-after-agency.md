# Postactivism After Agency

## Seed question

What remains of political and ethical action when the sovereign problem-solver is understood as part of the arrangement producing the problem?

## Possible center

[[bayo-akomolafe|Báyò Akómoláfé]]'s [[postactivism]] is not a program that follows activism. It names the breaks in which established problems, subjects, solutions, and scales of action no longer hold together. Its characteristic figures are cracks, slowing down, monsters, sanctuary, failure, fermentation, and becoming-with.

## Possible argument

Postactivism matters because it does not merely criticize ineffective tactics. It questions the ontological confidence that there is a stable world before an agent who can know, represent, and repair it. Its danger is the inverse: dispersed agency can become rhetorically exquisite while leaving responsibility for direct harms unspecified.

Reflexive temporal [[tending]] may offer a neighboring grammar. It agrees that the responder is within the field of response, but it does not dissolve the responding act. Inherited tendings become re-tendable; local agency remains real, situated, and answerable; effects are neither sovereignly produced nor washed into generalized entanglement.

## Necessary companions

- [[donna-haraway|Donna Haraway]] on response-ability and sympoiesis.
- [[vanessa-machado-de-oliveira|Vanessa Machado de Oliveira]] on complicity, maturity, and hospicing.
- Frantz Fanon or another figure of direct decolonial struggle as an adversarial interlocutor, so that postactivism must answer action under coercion rather than only solutionism under complexity.
