What is time? Wrong question, or at least the wrong first one. Ask instead what is happening. Right now. You, reading. Us, meeting in a sentence. This. Notice it for a moment before you go on. Something is happening, and it is not happening inside anything.

Here is the claim, and the rest of this essay is the bill for it. You are time. Or rather, since that word has been ruined by a picture, you are temporicity, a word that will be explained and that your body already understands.

The picture first, because it arrives before any answer does. Ask most people what time is and they reach for a tunnel, a container, a river, a line. Something you are in. Something that was there before you and will be there after, running on whether or not anyone is inside it. The picture is so old and so useful that it feels like perception. It is not. It is an image, and most of what puzzles us about time is really what puzzles us about the image. And notice what kind of image it is. A thing that was there before and will be there after, unchanged by anything that happens in it, is the oldest candidate there is for what nobody will let die. The container is time's [[what-this-work-does|death exemption]], and it sits so close in that it looks like sanity.

Start somewhere smaller than the universe. A musician does not have time; she keeps it. A breath keeps its own time through the taking, the turning, the release. A conversation keeps time in the way its silences and answers pace one another. A mountain has a time too, so slowly that we call it timeless, which only means its rhythm is not ours. Watch any happening closely and you do not find a thing sitting in time. You find an act with a pace, a rhythm, a while that belongs to that happening and no other.

This is the claim, and it is simpler than the river. A happening does not occur in time, because its happening is its time. Every happening spends itself, the way a breath or a fire or a life does. Every happening answers others, keeping time with them, sharing a rhythm, being held. And every happening stays open, not wholly gripped by what it was a moment ago. Spending, answering, staying open. Those three, together, in some proportion, are what it is for anything to have a time, and nothing holds them for it from outside. "Was," "is," and "will be" are how acts sound from inside other acts. The words are real. There is just no clock outside all the happenings for them to point at, only happenings keeping time with happenings.

And you. You are not in time. You are one of the things keeping it. Your body is a braid of rhythms, heart and breath and the slow turning of cells, none of them on a master clock, all of them answering one another. Your day is kept with other people's days. Your life is a while, and you are the one spending it. That is what it means to say you are temporicity. Not that you are made of time, as if time were a stuff. That you are one of the happenings the word time was only ever a name for.

Then what about the line? The timeline, the biography, history marching left to right. Here is where this account differs from the usual rebellions against clock-time. The line is real. It is just local. A story told, a plan laid, a calendar kept, a life remembered in order are genuine achievements, made by beings who share time and care where it is going. Sequence is something we do together, and we do it well. The mistake was never the line. The mistake was the promotion, which crowned a local human instrument as the form of time itself, so that what you inherited becomes "behind you," what you hope for "up ahead," and everything real is strung on one thread it never asked to hang from.

But if there is no line, does everything come loose? Can we go back? No, and the reason is the deepest fact this work knows. Every act happens [[the-once|once]]. What completes does not come back. Nothing can un-condition what conditioned it, and no happening can be its own ancestor. That is irreversibility, and notice that it never needed a line. One-way facts everywhere; no path anywhere. "Before" is ancestry, not an address.

And the clocks? The caesium atom, the atomic second, the astonishing agreement of instruments across the world? Clocks are not windows onto the container. They are coordination technology, rhythms so widely braided that any local ensemble can couple to them, the way an orchestra tunes to an oboe. The oboe is not the music's master; it is the habit everyone can hear. Every happening participates in many tempos at once, its cells' and its household's and its civilization's, nested rhythms with no master beat. The time is held between us, around here, by nobody and everybody.

Even the famous flow, time's river-feel, the slipping of it, survives this account, relocated. Nothing flows. Happenings spend, answer, and open, and some of them feel it. Beings like us, who keep long time together, thicken their keeping into memory, anticipation, the lived arrow. The flow is real the way music is real, as an achievement of ensembles, not a current under the world.

Here is where it turns, because a happening that can feel its time can also keep it on purpose. Most happenings have a time. A few [[tending|tend]] one. A mountain has its slow while and does nothing about it. You have yours and can. That is the whole difference between having a time and tending it, and it is not a difference between kinds of thing. It is a difference in what a happening does with what it is. You can let your time be kept for you, by the clock and the calendar and the people who expect yesterday's you. Or you can wake up inside it. Waking up is not a switch. It is a deepening, entered by living it and graded by how far in you go, and at the deep end it turns and notices itself. Temporicity waking up to itself. That is not a metaphor for something else this work believes in. It is what this work thinks you are, when you are most yourself.

It is also why time can be injured. When someone who kept your past with you and expected your future with you dies, the damage is not only to you. It is to the keeping. A rhythm that was held between two is now held by one, and the arrow that was shared is suddenly yours alone to carry. That is what grief is doing, and it is why it feels as though time itself has broken rather than a person having left. It is why joy is what it is, too. A keeping found. A rhythm answered. Time held between.

So what is time? The word names no thing. It is the family name for happenings keeping time together, the map-word for all the braided rhythms, and a good word so long as the map is not mistaken for the territory. If you want the territory, it is nearer than any definition. The next breath, kept, and kept on purpose if you like. The conversation, paced. The once of this very reading, already becoming what some later act will tend out of. This is the way it is, around here. As far as anyone can tell from inside a life, which is the only place anyone has ever told it from.

Listen to a room for a minute. Count nothing. There are more times in it than clocks in the world, and they are, all of them, keeping time.

## The argument, precisely

What follows is the same claim stated exactly, so that it can be checked, used, and caught drifting from itself. It is written to stand on its own.

### The claim, stated exactly

There is no Time. There are temporicities. A temporicity is a happening's own temporal character, per happening and per ecology, radically plural. Its structure is the happening's: three characters co-present in ratio, never in sequence and never at positions. Spending, the happening using itself up toward its completing. Answering, its time shared, entrained, held with others'. Release, its next moment not wholly gripped by its last. (Internally these are energeia, resonance, and aphesis.) Fullness is co-presence, not maximum. Every happening's time disperses, shares, and opens at once, and the extremes exclude one another. Every completing happens once and bears at its edge what was not there before (internally, apūrva, the not-before).

"Time," the word, is the map-word for temporicities coordinating. The container picture fails three tests at once. A container is reversible in principle; a temporicity is irreversible, because spending is. A container is unshared, each thing at its own coordinate; a temporicity is shared, because answering is. A container is fixed; a temporicity is open, because release is. That is how you know the container is a map mistaken for the territory. Clocks were always coordination technology among rhythms, never windows onto a container.

### Linearity, kept and demoted

Linearity is a real achievement and a local one. Where sequence-keeping and direction braid, in chronology, narrative, the calendar, a line is made, and it is useful. It is never constitutive of time. The error this work names is the promotion of a local instrument to the form of time itself. The reverse error is equally barred: no line is not no asymmetry. The once survives without a line. Irreversibility is a fact about dependence, not about geometry. Nothing un-conditions what conditioned it, and heirs cannot un-arise. "Before" is ancestry in a mesh, partial, local, plural, and never a coordinate. Descent cannot loop; the wider weave of influence can, and does.

### Plural pasts, plural not-yets

Each happening has its ancestry; each ecology has its settled. "The past," singular, is the timeline's freeze in temporal clothing, one record on permanent file. "The future" is nothing-yet that is not anywhere: unsettled, not unvisited. What has not yet completed has no fact yet, not merely no known fact. The unprecedented sits at every completing, not at the end of a road.

No face of a happening is the past, the present, or the future. Spending is not "the past" and openness is not "the future." Tense words are readings made from inside an ecology, real as readings and never the structure.¹ Tensed self-location, the sense of being here between a behind and an ahead, belongs to beings that tend their time, not to time.

### Having a time and tending it

Temporicity is universal. Every happening has its own temporal character, rock-events included, with nobody required. Tending is regional. Some happenings do something about their time: care for it, keep it on purpose. Tended temporicity, thickness, narrative, memory and anticipation, a life held as mine, is the enrichment of the universal by the region that tends, richer and never the genus. Felt flow is real as lived, an achievement of ecologies like ours, never a cosmic current. Nothing flows; happenings spend, answer, and open, and some of them feel it.

Waking up, in the face's sense, is consciousness as this work holds it. Entered by inhabiting: a rhythm of one's own, self-maintenance, mortal onces, aims that are held. Graded by depth. Reflexive at the deep end, where reflexivity is the deep form and not the definition. Consciousness is not a property some things possess and a container of awareness others lack. It is a happening's tending of its own time, deepened.

### Why the word

The coinage is deliberate. "Temporality" carries Heidegger's architecture with it: three ecstases unified in one horizon, a Dasein at the center, historicity downstream.² Temporicity refuses all three. No unified horizon, no privileged center, no History. The word is awkward enough to be safe, and it names what "time" cannot without the picture coming back.

### Scope, and what would show this wrong

As far as we can tell. The claim is made from the region that tends, in tenders' language, and it says so. Temporicities dominated by spending or by release are reached from here only adjacently, and beyond adjacency they are dark to us. This is the way it is, around here, and "around here" is the content, not the hedge.

Three things would count against it. A measurement that cannot be redescribed as coordination among rhythms, one that requires a container to make sense of it. A demonstrated master succession, one true line of inheritance to which every other line reduces. Or a happening that repeats, the once failing. None has been produced. The work would have to change if one were.

Consider this an invitation to answer.
