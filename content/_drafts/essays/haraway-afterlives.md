# After Haraway

## Seed question

What has happened to Donna [[donna-haraway|Haraway]]'s wager that knowledge is situated, species become together, and damaged worlds must be inhabited through response-ability rather than innocence?

## Possible cast

- [[anna-tsing|Anna Tsing]]: precarity, contaminated diversity, collaborative survival, and salvage accumulation.
- María Puig de la Bellacasa: matters of care and the material obligations of more-than-human worlds.
- Vinciane Despret: animals, questions, and the ways inquiry teaches beings how to answer.
- [[bayo-akomolafe|Báyò Akómoláfé]]: postactivism, cracks, sanctuary, and the decolonial migration of “staying with the trouble.”

## Possible claim

Haraway's largest afterlife is not a doctrine but a changed grammar of agency: knowledge becomes participation, response becomes reciprocal formation, and construction becomes making-with. The unresolved question is whether this grammar can discriminate among forms of relation strongly enough to guide action inside violent institutions.

## Relation to tending

The essay could distinguish sympoiesis from [[tending]]. Making-with describes constitutive co-production; tending additionally emphasizes directional care, finite completing, reflexive re-tending, plural temporicities, and the non-guaranteed arrival marked by [[apurva|apūrva]]. This is a divergence among neighboring projects, not a claim that Haraway anticipated the framework.
