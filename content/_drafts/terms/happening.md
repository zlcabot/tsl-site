A happening is a knot of tensions that happens and completes once.
It spends itself into this shape and no other, it answers what it
happens among, and it stands open at its own completing. All three
at once, always, in some proportion. A stone does this. A cell does
this. So does the sentence you are reading, and so does the reading
of it. Nothing underneath is doing the happening.

## Why this page exists

This work is easiest to misread in one particular way, and the
misreading was ours before it was anyone else's. For a long time the
writing here used [[tending]] for what every event does. But tending
is what a happening does when it comes to care, and care is not
everywhere. Using the reader's own verb for the shape of everything
is a description mistaking itself for the world.

So the two layers come apart. This page holds the universal one:
what a happening is, whether or not anybody is there for it.
[[tending]] holds the other, which is what some happenings make of
it.

## The three tensions

A tension here is not a strain and not a conflict. A body at rest is
as tensioned as a body under load. The word means a constitutive
holding, the way an act is held apart and together at the same time,
and each of the three names what is held.

_Intensity_ holds this happening with what it arises on. Something
spends itself into being determinately this, inheriting in order to
do it. This is the only one of the three that carries ancestry.
Arising runs one way, out of completings, and that is a
dependence-order rather than a timeline.

_Resonance_ holds together and different at once. Two strings tuned
to each other answer when either is struck, and the answering is
what keeps them two. It goes both ways or it is not answering.

_Openness_ holds the gripped with its release. There is room in a
happening that is not used up by whatever currently fills it, and it
is never exhausted by what it holds. It is a toward that puts
nothing later.

Three warnings, since each is a way readers reliably go wrong. These
are not stages. They are not parts. And they are emphatically not
past, present, and future, which is the A-series smuggled in through
the back door. There is no single time for them to run through.

## The knot is a ratio

What a happening has is not one of the three but a proportion among
them, read from somewhere. There is no such thing as an
intensity-event. A lean toward one character is a statement about
emphasis and never about membership, and raising one does not spend
another. The three are not a budget.

This is why the descriptions here always come in threes even when
only one is interesting at the moment. Drop one and you have not
described a simpler happening. You have described nothing.

## Three ways a knot holds

Tensions hold tensions, and the holdings take their character from
whichever tension leads. Naming them gives ordinary language
something to work with.

A holding that leans on intensity is an _entanglement_, and what
draws there is a bond. A holding that leans on resonance is an
_ecology_, and what draws there is a call. A holding that leans on
openness is a _participation_, and what draws there is an allure.
These shade into one another and none of them is a kind of thing.

The same pair of happenings can sit at different emphases depending
on which way you read it. An organ bonds out of its cells, so they
stand in its entanglement. The cells are drawn toward the organ, so
it stands in their participation. That is not two structures. It is
one holding, read from two places.

## Nobody required, and no whole

The tensions are the happening's own. A rock-event holds all three
with nobody home, and this is the line that keeps the work from
idealism: what is read is not made by the reading.

There is also no knot of everything. Any cut is made from
somewhere, and there is nowhere outside to make the last one from.
Many happenings hold at once, and a body, its cells, and its lineage
are not rival accounts of one true holding. Where a reading cuts is
ours. That a holding is there is not.

## The edge

Every happening completes, and at its completing something arrives
that no prior description supplied. That is not a fourth tension. It
is the edge of each one, and it has its own page: [[once|the once]].

## Relations

The regional counterpart is [[tending]], which is what a knot does
when it comes to care for the tensions it is. The three stretches a
tender makes are [[extending]], [[intending]], and [[attending]].
