The small set of words this work leans on. Each has one canonical
page that evolves in place and is never forked, dated, tended, and
linked from every use across the site.

The map has two layers, and the difference between them is the thing
most worth getting right. The upper layer is what every happening is,
whether or not anyone is there for it. The lower layer is what some
happenings do about it.

The upper layer, being written now: a happening is a knot of three
tensions that completes once. Intensity, the spending into this
shape. Resonance, the answering that keeps two things two. Openness,
the room that is never used up. Universal, and requiring nobody.

The lower layer, planted and tended:

- [[tending]]: what a happening does when it comes to care for the
  tensions it is. Regional, and where you are. Start here.
  - [[extending]], the extension: out of. Intensification, the
    eruption into form.
  - [[intending]], the care: with. Attunement, one shared while, two
    who stay two.
  - [[attending]], the orientation: toward. The held toward,
    direction before any plan.

Seeded and not yet planted: [[the-once]], where every act happens
once, and the page for the upper layer, which is in draft while its
name settles.

More are planted as the words settle.
