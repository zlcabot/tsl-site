The small set of words this work leans on. Each has one canonical
page that evolves in place and is never forked, dated, tended, and
linked from every use across the site. The indentation is the map:
the three ways belong to the one verb.

- [[tending]]: the master verb, to co-create what is happening.
  Start here.
  - [[extending]]: out of. Intensification, the eruption into form.
  - [[intending]]: with. The membrane, one shared while.
  - [[attending]]: toward. The arc, directionality before any plan.

Seeded and not yet planted: [[the-once]], where every act happens
once.

More are planted as the words settle.
