Attending is [[tending]] toward, the stretch a tender makes across the
openness it already is. Ordinary word: _orientation_. It names the
toward held open, the way an act stays directed before any plan, any
purpose, any watcher. A thought leans toward what it is about. A life
leans toward what calls it. This is directedness itself, and its
health is that it can let go of any particular target without losing
its direction.

• • • —

## The word

_Ad-tendere_: to tend toward. This is one of the three ways of
[[tending]], not the third of them, since the three are not a
sequence. English keeps the caring version everywhere: the attendant,
the attending physician, attending to a child, a guest, a dying
friend. It also keeps the spent version, attention as something paid.

The everyday word straddles two of the three ways, and it is worth
saying why rather than letting a reader trip on it. _Tend_ meaning
care for is a worn form of _attend_, so English hears caring inside
the toward. In this work's vocabulary those come apart. The with-ness
is called care and lives at [[intending]]; the toward is called
orientation and lives here. When you attend to a dying friend you are
doing both, which is the ordinary case and not a problem.

## What it names

Attending names the held toward. It is not a consciousness observing
from outside and not a goal already set. It is the act's own
directedness, and it is not a place the act is headed.

One distinction does the real work here. Direction of some kind runs
everywhere. A crystal grows one way rather than another, a slope
sheds water downhill, a gradient leans. Call that tendency: aimless,
unheld, nobody's. What a tender adds is the holding. Attending is
tendency taken up and kept open by a happening that has come to care
about where it leans. The crystal needs no awareness for its
direction to be real, and it is not thereby attending.

The toward is now. Direction is something an act does, not a place
it is going, and this is the guard the page exists for: attending is
not the future. A leaning puts nothing later. Read it as futurity and
the whole architecture quietly becomes a timeline with three
stations on it, which is exactly what this work refuses.

## Room, not reach

The positive thing attending contributes is easy to state backwards,
so state it forwards. Attending is not the way an act refuses to be
pinned down. It is the way an act has room.

A happening is never finally clamped by its own nature. Something in
it is not used up by whatever currently fills it, and that
unspent room is what lets a next thing be more than a repetition.
Letting-loose is a positive operation, as workaday as intensity or
answering, and it is neither passive nor a withdrawal. It is the
active leaving-open by which a direction stays a direction instead
of hardening into a track.

## The failure

Attending's characteristic failure is fixation. The toward seizes one
object and cannot release: the grip that mistakes itself for care,
the vigilance that has forgotten what it guards. Which reveals the
deep thing about attending's health, that it is as much release as
reach. A toward that can let go of any particular target without
losing direction, staying directed and warm and unclenched, is
attending at its fullest. Devotion that cannot release its object has
stopped being devotion and become possession.

## Relations

One of three ways of one act. Without [[extending]] there is nothing
formed to be directed, and without [[intending]] the toward has no
with to stay open in. The whole is at [[tending]].
