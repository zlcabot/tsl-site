Intending is [[tending]] with, the stretch a tender makes across the
resonance it already is. Ordinary word: _care_. It names
holding-with-difference, the answering by which happenings keep one
shared while and stay two in it. This is not the everyday "having a
plan" but something older and structural. Two strings do it. A real
conversation does it. Nothing happens alone.

• • • —

## The word

_In-tendere_: to tend in, to tend within. In everyday English
_intending_ has narrowed to mean having a purpose, as in "I intend
to call you." That is a late, specialized descendant. This work
restores the older sense, the with-ness of happening, the
relational way of the one verb.

## What it names

The picture to hold is attunement. Two strings tuned to each other
answer when either is struck, and the answering is exactly what
keeps them two. Nothing has merged. What has arrived is a shared
timing, a co-rhythm neither string owns, and it is real in the
answering rather than in either instrument. Take a conversation the
same way: not your words, not mine, but the between that makes them
answers to each other. That between is not a third thing standing
beside us, and it is not nothing. It is the conversation's own
while, a shared time that neither speaker owns.

A caution belongs here, because the obvious picture is the wrong
one. The living cell's membrane was long the favored image, and it
does show something true: a membrane is not a wall between two
things that were already there, and it constitutes inside and
outside as faces of one another. But a membrane is a structure, and
picturing the with-ness as a structure imports the very habit that
makes the with-ness invisible. What was seen in the cell was the
relating. What it was seen in was a thing.

The same substitution runs through the sciences whenever coupling
gets cashed out as what passes between: the transmitter, the ion,
the signal. Those are real and they are not the answering. They are
where the answering meets intensity, which is a boundary of the
with-ness and not the with-ness itself. Wherever different
happenings keep one while together, in a duet, a household, a
forest, intending is at work, and it will not be found by looking
for the substance that carries it.

## The failure

Intending's characteristic failure is fragmentation. The with lets
go, and what was held in difference falls into pieces that no
longer answer each other: the conversation that becomes two
monologues, the community that becomes a crowd. Its health is the
rarest of skills, holding difference without dissolving it,
with-ness that never requires sameness.

## Relations

One of three ways of one act. Without [[extending]] and
[[attending]] there is nothing distinct to hold and no toward for
the holding to keep open. The whole is at [[tending]].
