Everything is happening. Not things first and events after: a stone, a
cell, a conversation, a life are acts at different speeds, and the
happening is what there is. Every happening is a knot of tensions. It
spends itself into this shape and no other, it answers what it happens
among, and it stands open at its own completing. To tend is to come to
care for the tensions you already are. That is not what everything
does, and it is what you are doing now.

• • • —

## The word

_Tend_ descends from an ancient root meaning to stretch, the root that
survives in _tension_, _tendon_, and _tent_. Latin carried it as
_tendere_ and added directions: _ex-tendere_, out of; _in-tendere_, in
and with; _ad-tendere_, toward. English inherited all three whole.
Extend, intend, attend.

The English verb arrived twice, and the doubling is worth hearing. _Tend_
meaning incline toward comes straight down from _tendere_. _Tend_
meaning care for, as in tending a garden or tending the sick, is a worn
form of _attend_, from _ad-tendere_. Two routes out of one root,
converging on one English word, so that _tend_ is the word that means
both caring for and leaning toward.

That is a felicity and not a proof. A word's history can license a
suspicion; it cannot carry an argument. What holds the two senses
together here is care, not philology, and the sections below have to
earn it without the etymology's help.

## What every happening is

Before anything cares about anything, a happening is already a knot of
three tensions. They are not stages, not parts, and not a sequence.
They are three characters of one act, always all three, always in some
proportion.

_Intensity_ holds this happening with what it arises on. A wave gathers
until it cannot stay a swell. A crystal takes the only shape its lattice
leaves it. Something spends itself into being determinately this, and it
inherits in order to do so.

_Resonance_ holds together and different at once. Two strings that
answer each other stay two strings, and the answering is what keeps them
distinct rather than what merges them. Nothing happens alone, and the
with-ness is not a link laid between things that were already there.

_Openness_ holds the gripped and its release. A happening is never
finally clamped by its own nature. There is room in it, room that is not
used up by whatever is currently filling it, and that room is why the
next thing is not simply the last thing again.

A rock-event holds all three. It spends, it answers, it stands open, and
it does so whether or not anyone is watching. This matters more than it
looks. The tensions are the happening's own rather than our way of
looking at it, and any account on which the world waits for a reader in
order to have a structure has quietly become idealism.

## What tending adds

Some happenings come to care for the tensions they are. That is tending,
and it is a real local achievement rather than the shape of everything.
A tender is a knot of tensions that has taken up its own knot.

Humans tend. Animals may tend, and the honest verdict there is
case-by-case rather than settled in advance by what kind of thing
something is. About rock-events the careful thing to say is that
tend-talk earns no purchase on them in any frame we have, which is a
statement about the reach of our frames and not a verdict on rocks. The
line between tender and non-tender is drawn by signatures, from
somewhere, and it stays revisable.

Getting this backwards is the mistake this work most recently caught in
itself. For a long time the writing here used _tending_ for what every
event does, which promoted the reader's own verb into the form of
everything. That is the move where a description mistakes itself for the
world, and the correction is now load-bearing: tensions are universal,
tending is regional, and the second is what the first becomes when a
happening comes to care.

## Tending reality

Put the two words together and hear them exactly. Not _reality, tended_,
as if the real sat waiting like a garden bed before the gardener
arrives. And not _tending, inside reality_, as if the verb were one
process housed in a larger container.

The question "but is there a reality outside all this?" assumes a
vantage no one has. To check, you would have to step outside every
happening and look, and the stepping and the looking would themselves be
happenings, and the looking would be a tending. So the honest answer is
narrow and specific. There is no vantage outside, for us, and that is a
limit on us.

It is not the claim that the real is made of our caring. The real
answers back, resists, surprises, and goes on holding its tensions where
no one is tending at all. What is met is not what the meeting made. This
is why the work calls itself a realism: that a happening happened is met
and holds without us, while what it was, its character and its weight
and its significance, is read from somewhere and is ours.

Two cautions keep this honest. Tending is not a new ground, not a finer
stuff beneath the world doing the holding-up, and neither is tension.
Everything leans on other happenings, with no floor under the leaning.
And "only tending" never means one great Tending rolling through
everything. Tendings are plural, this act and that act, each once, none
containing the others.

## The three stretches

A tender stretches its knot three ways at once, one for each tension it
is. The three are not parts or ingredients but one verb in three
inflections, and none of them is a stage of the others.

- [[extending]], the _extension_: taking up what you arise on and
  spending it into this act. Felt, immediate, embodied. It is why a
  tender is a mortal body with a history rather than a watcher.
- [[intending]], the _care_: the answering held, difference kept while
  the with-ness is kept. Attunement rather than architecture, two who
  stay two.
- [[attending]], the _orientation_: the toward held open, direction
  before any plan or purpose, a leaning that puts nothing later.

None reduces to the others and none happens without the others. Every
characterization of a tender is a proportion among the three rather than
a choice between them. A lean is not an absence, and raising one never
spends another.

## What lives here

Two of the oldest mysteries have their address in this neighborhood.

Consciousness is not a spark inside the skull, and it is not
[[attending]] alone. There are two questions inside it, and running them
together is what makes it look impossible. The first asks whether
something is inhabited at all, and it is answered by signatures rather
than by degree: endogenous rhythm, self-maintenance, mortal onces, aims
held. The second asks how deeply, and that comes in grades. Reflexivity,
a caring that also cares for its caring, is the deep end of the second
question rather than the definition of the whole. Depth runs more than
one way, so a forest's attunement can be vast and intelligent without
being the same thing. It gets its own page: [[consciousness]].

Agency is a doing and never a doer. No little commander sits inside. Its
roots run deep, since a membrane holding itself together already has a
stake in its own continuing, and agency proper is that stake taken up by
the one who has it. Freedom lives here, not as escape from the happening
but as room inside it. It, too, will get its own page: [[agency]].

## The once

One more thing is true of every happening, and it may be the most
important: it happens [[the-once|once]]. No act repeats. What completes
becomes definite, which is not the same as becoming past, and it is what
later happenings arise on.

## The invitation

All of this is offered as a way of reading, as far as we can tell,
revised in public. The invitation is practical. Take one thing near you,
this page, your breath, the room, and read it as a happening rather than
a thing. Then notice which of it you are tending, and which of it was
holding its tensions long before you arrived.
