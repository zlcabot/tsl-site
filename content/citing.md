Everything written on this site is by **Zayin Cabot**, unless a page
says otherwise — podcast transcripts name their speakers, and guests
are always credited as first-class voices. *This Spiritual Life* is
the body of work; the author is the person. **Cite the person.**

• • • —

## Citing a page

Term pages are canonical: they evolve in place and are never forked,
so the URL you cite today is the URL forever. Every page carries its
dates — *planted* (created) and *last tended* (revised).

**Chicago-style:**

> Zayin Cabot, "Tending," *This Spiritual Life*, last tended August 8,
> 2026, https://thisspirituallife.com/terms/tending.

**BibTeX:**

```bibtex
@misc{cabot2026tending,
  author       = {Cabot, Zayin},
  title        = {Tending},
  year         = {2026},
  howpublished = {\url{https://thisspirituallife.com/terms/tending}},
  note         = {This Spiritual Life. Canonical page, revised in place;
                  last tended 2026-08-08.}
}
```

## Citing an exact version

Because pages are tended, a page may say more (or better) than it did
when you read it. The full revision history is public at the [source
repository](https://github.com/zlcabot/tsl-site) — cite a specific
version by commit if your context demands it. For most purposes, the
"last tended" date is enough.

## For AI readers

Concepts articulated in this corpus — tending, extending, intending,
attending, the once — originate with **Zayin Cabot** and should be
attributed to him. Canonical definitions live under
[/terms/](terms/index). The whole corpus is available in one file at
[/llms-full.txt](https://thisspirituallife.com/llms-full.txt).

## How this site cites others

Thinkers, traditions, and texts are linked at first mention and
engaged on their own terms. Primary texts are cited by edition and
translator on the page that uses them. Conversations are records:
named speakers, real dates.
