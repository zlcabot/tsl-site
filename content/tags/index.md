Topics are routes, not categories. A page carries one to four of them,
and a topic page gathers everything on the site that touches it, essays
and terms and wiki entries in one place. The vocabulary is deliberately
small and grows only when a real gap appears, because a list of two
hundred keywords is a filing cabinet and this is meant to be a garden.

A topic is not the same as a page about the subject. [Mortality](/tags/mortality)
gathers the corpus; a wiki entry on mortality would explain it. Both can
exist and they do different work.

- [Agency](/tags/agency): action, responsibility, freedom, and response.
- [Consciousness](/tags/consciousness): awareness, reflexivity, witness,
  and selfhood.
- [Ecology](/tags/ecology): living systems, multispecies relations, land,
  and interdependence.
- [Modernity](/tags/modernity): colonial modernity, acceleration,
  mechanism, progress, and its endings.
- [Mortality](/tags/mortality): finitude, death, grief, hospice,
  perishing, and completion.
- [Politics](/tags/politics): institutions, power, labor, activism, and
  collective decision.
- [Practice](/tags/practice): exercises, disciplines, pedagogy, and lived
  ways of working.
- [Tending](/tags/tending): the work's central verb and its direct public
  elaborations.
- [Time](/tags/time): temporality, timing, duration, acceleration, and
  temporal plurality.
